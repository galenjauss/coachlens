# CoachLens demo script (2:30)

Record through-the-lens footage if possible, plus the phone screen. Use the seeded fake students and the owned demo inbox. Confirm before recording: iPhone on charge, Do Not Disturb on, API warm (open `https://matside-api.vercel.app/health` once; cold start is about 6 s), mode set to Martial arts, a class started from the lens.

| Time | Shot | Say / do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:12 | Instructor with pads, kids in the background (or B-roll). Title card with numbers. | "I teach kids martial arts alone. Twelve kids, hands on pads, and I forget who I corrected. High-information feedback is four times as effective as praise (d=0.99 vs 0.24)." |
| 0:12-0:22 | Phone: onboarding quiz, speed-through. Paste a schedule, show the extracted tables, tap confirm. | "Setup is a ten-second quiz. Paste a schedule or snap a sign-in sheet; AI fills the roster." |
| 0:22-0:32 | Phone: switch modes (Martial arts, Personal training, Tutoring) and show the labels change. | "One engine, three kinds of teaching. Same agents, different vocabulary and guardrails." |
| 0:32-0:50 | Lens: home, tap, "Everyone except Jake and Mia". Card: `12 present / absent: Jake, Mia`. | "Roll call is one sentence." |
| 0:50-1:00 | Lens: drill card, timer ticking, `Next feedback: Priya`. | "The lens shows the drill, the time, and who has not had feedback." |
| 1:00-1:25 | Lens: tap, say "Priya, guard drops on the jab again, great energy though", tap. Show the two-column card filling: `✓ sorted`, `✓ logged`, `… email`, `✓ plan`. | "No commands. I just talk. Agents log it, update tonight's plan, and tell me what they did." |
| 1:25-1:45 | Lens: tap, say "Leo got hit in the face, he's okay but his dad should hear it". Card shows the urgent line. Phone: Agents grid tile turns red. | "Urgent notes draft a parent email immediately. Routine ones don't spam parents; they go on the monthly report card." |
| 1:45-2:00 | Phone: Review, open Leo's email, type in "Tell this agent..." "warmer, shorter", watch it redraft, tap Send. Cut to the inbox with the email arriving. | "Every email waits for my approval, and I can steer the agent in plain words." |
| 2:00-2:12 | Phone: Students, Priya, "Run report cards", show October's card with quoted coach notes. | "Monthly, each parent gets a report card grounded in what I actually said in class." |
| 2:12-2:22 | Lens: nod to go dark, tap to wake, double-tap, confirm; wrap-up `Feedback 9/12`, missed list. | "At the end, I see who I missed, and they go first next class." |
| 2:22-2:30 | Title card: "CoachLens. Prep Notes for the dojo." Repo and Beta install info. | "Teach the class. CoachLens runs the business." |

## Before you record
- Billing line is optional if time is tight: "Leo's dad still owes for sparring gear, kinda urgent" gives `✓ Billing flag · Leo · HIGH`. It reinforces "flags only, no payments."
- If the nod misfires on camera, skip it and use the tap-to-wake only.
- Keep one safety take: the phone screen recorded through the iOS screen recorder, in case through-the-lens footage is too dim.

## At the table (live)
Let judges wear the G2 and try: roll call, one spoken note, the agent card. Pairing code is given in person.
