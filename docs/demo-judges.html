You're the coach. Teach a class, talk about your students.

How to use the glasses

Use the touchpad on the glasses arm. Start on the class list: swipe to pick a class, tap to open it, tap again to start class.

TapStart a note, then talkTap again when you're done speaking
Double-tapUndo the last noteWithin 6 seconds, while its result card is showing
SwipeSee today's drillsOr open a ★ insight when one appears
Double-tapEnd classFrom the class screen, then tap to confirm

Screen went dark? That's on purpose: the lens stays dark when nothing is happening. Just tap.

Things to say (tap first)

  1. Everyone except Mia.
    Takes attendance. Watch for a ★ — Jake just came back after missing 3.
  2. Priya keeps dropping her guard.
    Her third guard note, so it becomes a focus in her next class's plan.
  3. Priya kept her guard up that time.
    Praise stays praise. Double-tap right away to undo it.
  4. Dee rolled her ankle and sat out the last drill.
    Drafts an email to Dee's parents. It waits for the coach to approve.
  5. Who keeps dropping their guard?
    Ask the whole class a question.
  6. How's Priya doing?
    Her rank, attendance and recent notes.

Then pick up the phone: Review has the drafted emails, Today shows what the agents noticed, Agents sets each one to Auto, Review or Off.
